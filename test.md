Pondicherry, officially renamed ⁠Puducherry, is a charming coastal 
union territory located on the southeastern Coromandel Coast of 
India. Known as the "French Riviera of the East", the city uniquely 
blends its distinct French colonial heritage—visible in mustard-colored 
mustard villas, boulevards, and chic cafes of White Town—with vibrant 
South Indian traditions. Visitors are drawn to its serene Promenade Beach, 
the spiritual ambiance of the Sri Aurobindo Ashram, and a tranquil 
lifestyle that offers an escape from fast-paced city life.
